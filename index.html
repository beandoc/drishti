Advanced Procedures

Our Treatments

Vitrectomy

A precision surgery to remove part or all of the vitreous humor. Anterior vitrectomy addresses vitreous issues in the front structures of the eye, often necessary when tangled with intraocular lenses.

Cornea & Refractive

Comprehensive care for conditions affecting the cornea and refractive errors caused by the shape of the eye, restoring clarity and precision to your vision.

Oculoplasty

Specialized plastic surgery for eyes and surrounding structures, improving tear drainage, correcting eyelid malposition, and treating skin cancers of the eyelid.

Retinal Disorders

Light-sensitive tissue care targeting the back of the eye to prevent vision loss and blindness caused by complex retinal conditions.
